# Settlement economy

*По-русски: [economy.ru.md](economy.ru.md)*

## Town resources
Every settlement runs six stocks (visible in the town panel — **H** key):

| Resource | Comes from | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| **Coins** (treasury) | production, trade, player sales, war loot | upkeep, hiring guards, mustering armies, construction, roads |
| **Food** | farms, hunting lodges, food expeditions, cooking | feeding the garrison, recruit rations, army provisions |
| **Raw meat** | hunters, scavenged battlefields | the kitchen cooks it into food |
| **Wood** | lumber yards, logging expeditions | repairing buildings and palisades, construction |
| **Metal** | mines, mining expeditions | the smithy discounts army musters |
| **Goods** | trade stalls, bakery (mill + 2 food → pastry), donations | caravan trade |

Stocks are not just numbers: surplus is deposited as **real items** into storehouse
chests. Taking them is theft — your reputation collapses and the garrison turns
hostile.

## How towns grow
Paid upkeep accumulates **prosperity** → the town climbs tiers:
outpost → village → town → capital. Each tier means more buildings, garrison and
shopkeepers. Role buildings grant bonuses: a mill boosts farms (+50% food), a
barracks speeds musters and stars-up recruits, a storehouse raises stock targets,
a trade stall raises income.

## How factions make a living
- **Civilised factions** (vikings, dvergr): the full cycle — workers gather,
  caravans trade along built roads, smithies forge. The archetype sets the slant:
  traders are coin-rich, farmers food-rich, miners metal-rich, warriors army-rich.
- **Raiders** sit outside the forge economy: they don't craft or build roads.
  They live off plunder — stripping every corpse on their land, robbing caravans,
  sacking towns. Hunger doesn't kill them, it **drives** them: an empty larder
  speeds up the horde's muster. The more they are attacked, the richer they get.

## Trading as a player
- The **shopkeeper** in a civilised town sells goods at reputation-based prices.
- The **buying post** (a kiosk with a chest): drop resources in — the town takes
  them into stock and leaves coins at the deficit price.
- The **gift chest**: donate anything — the town digests it into stocks and your
  reputation grows.
